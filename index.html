Dagu: Kids ABC & 123 — Privacy Policy

Developer: NOZIKSTUDIO · Contact: Nozikstudio@gmail.com · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Dagu is a learning game for young children (colors, numbers and letters). We take children's privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way for children to enter personal data.

Game progress (stars), the chosen language and the sound setting are saved only on the device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. All ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA) and as under the age of consent (GDPR), and are limited to content rated for general audiences ("G"). This means:

  • No personalized or interest-based ads and no remarketing.
  • The app does not access the Android Advertising ID.
  • Google may process limited technical data (for example IP address, device type and app information) only to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, as described in Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads are shown only on the main menu and occasionally between rounds — never at app start and never while a child is answering.

3. In-app purchase ("Remove ads")

Parents can remove all ads with a one-time purchase. The purchase screen is protected by a parental gate (a math question for adults). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive payment card or billing details.

4. Voice

Spoken instructions use the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. Text is processed on the device. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

5. Links

Links leaving the app (such as this policy) are only available in the parents' area, behind the parental gate.

6. Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information about your child has been collected, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: Nozikstudio@gmail.com
